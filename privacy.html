Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Facet does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your focus sessions, task names and settings are stored only on your device, inside the app. They are removed when you delete the app.

Exports

Images and videos you create are generated on your device and are shared only when you choose to share them through the iOS share sheet.

Network

Facet works fully offline. The app makes no network requests.

Children

Facet collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue.
