Facet: Focus Crystal Timer

A gentle focus timer. One thing, one crystal.

How it works

Pick a length, tap Start focus, and a unique crystal grows while you work. Stopping early is fine: the crystal stays as far as it grew. After a session, a two-minute reset game helps you take a calm break. The Today tab shows every crystal you grew.

Common questions

  • Where is my data? Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • How do I save a crystal? On the finish screen, tap Save image or Timelapse video, then use the share sheet.
  • Does Facet need an account? No.
